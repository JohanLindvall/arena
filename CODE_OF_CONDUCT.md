# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to ask questions, report problems, and contribute,
regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## Working together

- Be respectful and patient. Explain assumptions and make room for people
  learning Go or the project.
- Discuss the code and the evidence. Give specific, constructive feedback
  and accept corrections.
- Respect boundaries and privacy. Ask before sharing another person's
  personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual attention, personal
attacks, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Retaliation
against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

These expectations apply in the project's issues, pull requests, and other
project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and response

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainer, Johan Lindvall, at
[johan.lindvall@gmail.com](mailto:johan.lindvall@gmail.com). Include links or
context when possible. You do not need to confront the person involved or
make a public report.

The maintainer will review reports and share personal details only as needed
to address the concern. Depending on the behavior and its impact, the
response may include a request to change behavior, removal of content, or a
temporary or permanent restriction on participation.

For vulnerabilities in the software, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
